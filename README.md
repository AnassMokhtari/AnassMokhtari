## About me

I am an **Embedded Systems and Robotics engineer**, recently graduated from the **Faculty of Science and Technology, Al Hoceima**, with a strong focus on **automotive embedded software validation**, **system testing**, and **automation**.

My main interests are centered around **automotive software testing**, **FOTA validation**, **diagnostics**, and **HIL-based validation workflows**. During my final-year internship at **Lear Corporation**, I worked as a **System Test Engineer intern** on an internal automated **Firmware Over-The-Air Testing Tool**, combining **CANoe**, **CAPL**, **Python**, **UDS**, **ISO-TP**, and **HIL bench testing**.

I care about building systems that are not only functional, but also **structured, traceable, testable, and easy to debug**. I enjoy working at the intersection of embedded systems, automotive communication protocols, test automation, and real-world validation environments.
